## 2. My findings

### Finding 1 - Animated banner shows a sunny day all the time
- Where: https://catchmybusnew.vercel.app/, Live Arrivals screen, the animated banner at the top of the page
- What I did, what I saw: I opened the app. The banner shows a bright sun regardless of the real weather, even when the weather panel below says it is raining.
- Which heuristic: 2 - Match Between the System and the Real World. The banner always shows the sun even if the real weather is rain.
- Screen or system: SCREEN, as the banner is a standalone animation and the page already receives the live forecast from /api/rain that it could use instead.
- Severity, and why: 3, driven by whether it damages the product's standing out of proportion. The sun is the first thing a user sees, and it gives a false impression that the weather is always sunny, which undermines trust in the weather panel below.
- The repair: The banner either omits the sun and weather, or shows an icon that matches the live forecast for the selected area.

### Finding 2 - Invalid bus stop code such as "99999" looks like "no buses" instead of "no such bus stop"
- Where: https://catchmybusnew.vercel.app/, Live Arrivals screen, stop code box
- What I did, what I saw: I typed 99999 and pressed Show buses. The message shown was "No bus services at bus stop 99999 right now!! Check the 5-digit code on the bus stop pole, or try again after 5:30 am." After pressing Show buses several more times, the message changed to "Bus stop code is invalid!! The 5 digit code is printed on the pole at the bus stop."
- Which heuristic: 9 - Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from Errors
- Screen or system: SYSTEM. The back end (api/bus.js) asks LTA for buses, LTA sends back an empty list for a fake stop, and the back end passes that on as "success, zero buses". The screen has no way to know the stop was fake.
- Severity, and why: 3, driven by whether the person can learn around it. When someone types a stop code that does not exist, my app shows the same message as a real stop with no buses, so the user cannot tell "I typed it wrong" from "no buses right now" and it catches them every time.
- The repair: When a bus stop code that does not exist is entered, the message says the code was not found ("Bus stop code is invalid!! The 5 digit code is printed on the pole at the bus stop."), keeps what was typed, and looks different from a real stop that simply has no buses right now.

### Finding 3 - Cannot bookmark a favourite bus stop, only individual buses
- Where: https://catchmybusnew.vercel.app/, Live Arrivals screen, the star beside each bus service
- What I did, what I saw: I wanted to star or favourite the whole bus stop, but there is no star on the stop itself, only on each bus service, so I had to tap every star one by one.
- Which heuristic: 7 - Flexibility and Efficiency of Use
- Screen or system: SCREEN, as the page already has the stop and its services; it only lacks a button to save the stop as a whole.
- Severity, and why: 1, driven by whether the person can learn around it. It costs a few extra taps once, and after that the favourites work normally.
- The repair: The user can save a bus stop as a favourite with one tap, instead of tapping the star on every bus at that stop.

### Finding 4 - Bus arrival information is far down the page on a phone screen
- Where: https://catchmybusnew.vercel.app/, Live Arrivals screen, on a phone
- What I did, what I saw: I searched for Orchard, chose a bus stop on Orchard Road, and wanted to find the arrival time for Bus 77. It took several scrolls past the banner, weather panel, stop code box and search box to reach the arrival list.
- Which heuristic: 8 - Aesthetic and Minimalist Design
- Screen or system: SCREEN, as the page already has all the information; it is the order and size of the elements on the page that pushes the arrivals down.
- Severity, and why: 2, driven by what it costs when it happens. It is still usable, but a commuter at the stop has to scroll for too long to find the one thing they came for.
- The repair: On opening the app on a phone, the arrival times for the chosen stop are visible within one screen without scrolling past the banner and weather panel.

### Finding 5 - No default weather area for the chosen stop; the user must map their stop to data.gov.sg's area name
- Where: https://catchmybusnew.vercel.app/, Live Arrivals screen, weather panel ("Forecast for the City area", "Select forecast area" dropdown)
- What I did, what I saw: I chose a bus stop near my location. The weather panel still showed the City area, and I had to pick the area from the dropdown myself. I may not know which data.gov.sg area covers my bus stop. I would like the app to show the live weather and the time window of the forecast for my current location.
- Which heuristic: 2 - Match Between the System and the Real World
- Screen or system: SYSTEM. The screen has the stop's coordinates from /api/stop and the area names from /api/rain, but it is never sent the coordinates of each weather area, so it cannot work out which area the stop belongs to. That mapping has to be done in api/rain.js, with a small screen change afterwards to name the area in plain words.
- Severity, and why: 3, driven by how often it happens. Every user meets it on every visit unless they change the dropdown, and most users will not know the data.gov.sg area name for their stop.
- The repair: The forecast shown is for the area nearest the chosen stop, in one plain sentence such as "Light rain expected between 5:30 pm and 7:30 pm (updated 5:25 pm)", with the dropdown kept only as an override.

### Finding 6 - Search only works with LTA's abbreviations
- Where: https://catchmybusnew.vercel.app/, Live Arrivals screen, "Search by bus stop description or road name" box
- What I did, what I saw: I searched "opposite bugis" and there were no results. When I searched "opp bugis", the Opp Bugis Stn bus stop appeared. The same happened with "North Bridge Road" (no results) versus "Nth Bridge Rd" (results shown).
- Which heuristic: 6 - Recognition Rather than Recall
- Screen or system: SYSTEM. When I type "North Bridge Road", the screen sends it to /api/stop?q=North Bridge Road. The back end (api/stop.js) looks for that exact text inside LTA's descriptions and road names. LTA writes "Nth Bridge Rd", so nothing matches and the back end returns an empty list. The screen just shows what it was given: "No matching bus stops found".
- Severity, and why: 3, driven by how often it happens. Anyone who searches in everyday words meets it, and the "no results" message wrongly suggests the stop does not exist.
- The repair: Typing a place or road name in everyday words (North Bridge Road, Opposite Bugis Junction) finds the same stops as LTA's abbreviated spelling.

### Finding 7 - The bus in the animated banner appears to move backwards
- Where: https://catchmybusnew.vercel.app/, Live Arrivals screen, the animated banner at the top of the page
- What I did, what I saw: I opened the app and watched the banner. The bus appears to be driving in reverse.
- Which heuristic: 2 - Match Between the System and the Real World
- Screen or system: SCREEN, as this is a standalone animation.
- Severity, and why: 1, driven by whether it damages the product's standing out of proportion. It looks odd but does not affect any task.
- The repair: The bus in the banner moves forward.
